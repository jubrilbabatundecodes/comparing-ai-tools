## Our Commitment

Three principles guide every comparison we publish:

1. **Scores are independent.** Payment never buys a score, verdict, or placement. If a link earns us a commission, we disclose that relationship; it does not change the rating.

2. **Evidence is traceable.** Scores draw on cited public benchmarks, community feedback, official documentation, or hands-on testing that we disclose.

3. **The method is visible.** Articles and our [About](/about/) page explain the scoring framework, test approach, and any weight changes. Readers can see how each weighting affects the final score.

## How Articles Are Created

### Selection
We select comparisons by looking at:
- Search demand (are people actively comparing these two tools?)
- Decision difficulty (is the choice non-obvious?)
- Data availability (can we find enough reliable information to make a meaningful comparison?)

### Research
Research for each article may include:
- **Public benchmarks:** LMSYS Chatbot Arena, HumanEval, SWE-bench, Video Arena, and industry evaluations
- **Community consensus:** Reddit, Hacker News, official forums, professional communities
- **Official documentation:** Pricing pages, technical docs, release notes
- **Hands-on testing:** Identical prompts run across tools and compared side-by-side

If an assessment relies on benchmarks or community feedback rather than our own hands-on testing, we say so in that article's "Data Sources" section.

### Review
- We fact-check articles before publication
- We confirm listed prices against official sources
- We compare scores with relevant benchmark data when available

## Updates

| Update Type | Frequency |
|-------------|-----------|
| **Pricing** | Quarterly, or when announced |
| **Scores** | When major model updates are released |
| **New data** | As reliable benchmarks and community data become available |
| **Corrections** | Within 48 hours of receiving a verified correction |

The date labeled "Last updated" at the end of an article marks its latest substantive revision.

## Corrections

Found something that needs correcting?
- [File a GitHub issue](https://github.com/jubrilbabatundecodes/ComparingAItools/issues) with the article link and the correction
- We check verified reports and update the article within 48 hours
- We record significant corrections at the end of the article

## Independence

- **Funding:** We operate independently and earn revenue through affiliate commissions and (future) display advertising.
- **No pay-to-play:** No one can buy a score or influence our verdicts through payment.
- **Transparent methodology:** When our scoring approach changes, we update the public framework and explain the revision.
