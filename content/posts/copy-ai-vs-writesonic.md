## Three Head-to-Head Tests 🔬

### Test 1: Sales Copy

**Task:** Build a complete outbound sequence for one B2B product: cold email, follow-up, LinkedIn message, and call script. **Copy.ai:** Its GTM workflow produced all four from a product brief, keeping the message consistent while adapting the tone to each channel. **Writesonic:** The individual assets were good, but keeping them aligned required a manual review.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Copy.ai — GTM workflows are its standout feature.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: SEO Content

**Task:** Produce a 1,500-word SEO-focused blog post and analyze its keywords. **Writesonic:** Supplied keyword suggestions, an automatically generated meta description, and built-in SEO scoring, reaching 78/100. **Copy.ai:** Wrote solid copy, but offers no SEO tooling, so optimization depends on an external tool.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Writesonic — integrated SEO is its advantage.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: How Much the Platform Covers

Writesonic also includes Botsonic: train a chatbot on your documents, then deploy it on a website. Copy.ai has no equivalent, but offers stronger brand-voice controls and sales workflows. Writesonic spans more use cases; Copy.ai goes deeper on GTM.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Writesonic for breadth (SEO + chatbot); Copy.ai for GTM depth.</strong></p></div>
