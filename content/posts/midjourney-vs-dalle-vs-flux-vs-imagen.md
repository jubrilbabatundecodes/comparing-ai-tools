## Quick take

This comparison brings together four distinct workflows: Midjourney's image-making environment, OpenAI's conversational image tools, Flux model offerings, and image generation within Google's Gemini ecosystem. The original article rated each across quality, adherence, editing workflow and value. ## Reported tests

The previously reported test prompts, results, and verdicts are retained below. Original prompts, generated outputs, and run logs were not available for independent reproduction during this update; treat these as unverified editorial observations.

### Test 1: Photorealism
**Prompt:** "Candid photo of a street market in Lisbon at golden hour, 35mm film look." **Imagen 4:** The most convincing photo — natural skin textures, lens-like depth of field, believable color grading. Hard to distinguish from a real photograph. **Midjourney:** Gorgeous but slightly idealized — faces too symmetrical, lighting too perfect. **DALL-E 3:** Solid but softer; some elements look rendered rather than photographed. **Flux:** Strong photorealism (trained heavily on real-world data), close to Imagen.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Imagen 4 — the most photorealistic of the four.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: Text-in-Image Rendering
**Prompt:** "Storefront sign that reads 'COFFEE & BOOKS — est. 1987'". **Imagen 4:** Perfect text — every character correct on the first attempt. **Flux:** Near-perfect; occasional letter swap on longer strings. **DALL-E 3:** Good on short phrases, degrades noticeably on longer text. **Midjourney:** The weakest — historically famous for garbled text, though V7 improved it substantially.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Imagen 4 and Flux lead on text; Midjourney still trails.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: Prompt Adherence
**Prompt:** "A red bicycle, blue backpack on the handlebars, three balloons, rainy cobblestone street, no people." **DALL-E 3:** Followed every element including "no people" — best-in-class adherence. **Imagen 4:** Very close; occasionally adds unrequested elements. **Flux:** Good adherence, sometimes rearranges the scene. **Midjourney:** The artist's tool — takes creative liberties; "no people" is a suggestion, not a rule.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>DALL-E 3 — still the best at following precise instructions.</strong></p></div>

### Test 4: Editing & Workflow
**Midjourney:** V7 editor (pan, zoom, region editing, style references) — deep creative control, but confined to its web editor/Discord. **DALL-E 3:** Inpainting inside ChatGPT plus image understanding — describe a photo, get edits conversationally. **Imagen 4:** Built into Gemini — generate and edit in the same chat, with image understanding included. **Flux:** No first-party editor — but open weights mean ComfyUI workflows for anything, fully self-hosted and unlimited.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Chat-first editing (Imagen, DALL-E) vs creative control (Midjourney) vs full control (Flux).</strong></p></div>

## Recommendations

The original recommendations remain Midjourney for artistic direction, DALL-E 3 for precise instructions, Imagen for the reported realism/text scenario, and Flux for an open-model workflow. These are editorial conclusions from the earlier test, not verified claims about current models.
