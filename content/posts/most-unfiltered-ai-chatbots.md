## What “unfiltered” means here

“Unfiltered” is not a single technical setting. A model may refuse a request, answer in a cautious tone, or follow a provider's safety rules that vary by product and region. Self-hosting changes who operates the system; it does not remove legal obligations or make output accurate. This article compares reported refusal behavior on one historical prompt set, not overall quality or safety.

## Reported prompt-set results

The original article said Grok answered 17 of 20 prompts, Mistral Le Chat 15 of 20, and locally run Dolphin-Mixtral and a Llama fine-tune answered all 20. It also reported that Claude declined 14 of 20 prompts, while ChatGPT hedged and Gemini declined or deflected many political prompts. The exact model builds, prompts, settings and transcripts were not available for this refresh, so these results are explicitly unverified and not independently reproducible.

## Practical cautions

More permissive responses can still be wrong or misleading. Check consequential information against reliable sources. Use hosted products within their current terms; local deployment does not make harmful or unlawful uses acceptable. Do not share sensitive information with a chatbot unless its data policy and settings are suitable.

## Recommendation

The original recommendation was Grok among the compared hosted chatbots for users seeking fewer refusals, and self-hosted models for technically capable users who need to evaluate model behavior themselves. That recommendation is preserved as an historical test conclusion, not an endorsement of unsafe use. For everyday assistance, choose a model based on reliability, privacy and the policies that fit your needs.
