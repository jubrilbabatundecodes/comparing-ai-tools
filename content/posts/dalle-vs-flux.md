## Three Head-to-Head Tests 🔬

<div class="source-citation">
  <strong>Data Sources:</strong> Official documentation (OpenAI, Black Forest Labs), community comparisons (r/StableDiffusion, r/dalle2, Civitai, X/Twitter), our own testing. See <a href="/posts/flux-vs-midjourney/">Flux vs Midjourney</a> and <a href="/posts/midjourney-vs-dalle3/">Midjourney vs DALL-E 3</a> for broader context.

### Scenario 1: Photorealism & Quality

**Test method:** "A weathered fisherman on a dock at golden hour, editorial photography, 85mm f/1.4, every wrinkle and pore visible."

Flux's image looked strikingly real: the fisherman's skin, dock grain, and golden-hour warmth all read as photographic. Its photorealism score of 9.0 is closer to Midjourney's 9.4 than DALL-E 3's 8.0. That is an unprecedented quality level for an open-source model.

DALL-E 3's image was attractive and well composed, though it retained a subtle synthetic look: more saturated color, flatter shadows, and less organic texture. It looks like polished AI art; Flux comes closer to a photograph.

<div class="verdict-box">
  <div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div>
  <p class="verdict-text">
    <strong>Winner: Flux (9.0 vs 8.0).</strong> Realism is the widest gap between them. Flux can pass for a photograph, while DALL-E 3 looks like exceptionally good AI art.
  </p>
</div>

### Scenario 2: Prompt Adherence & Text Rendering

**Test method:** "A wooden bowl containing exactly 4 red apples and 2 green apples, on a marble counter, morning sunlight, shallow depth of field. Also: a minimalist logo for 'Nexus Technologies' — clean, modern, blue and white."

DALL-E 3 produced the requested 4 red and 2 green apples with accurate placement and lighting. Its "Nexus Technologies" logo looked clean and modern, with the name correctly spelled and integrated into the design. ChatGPT's automatic prompt rewrite helped translate the description into precise image instructions.

Flux also spelled the logo correctly, with text rendering well ahead of Midjourney's. It matched the apple count (4 red, 2 green), but was a little less exact than DALL-E on composition, including the lighting angle and focus. Both performed well with text—a first for this kind of image comparison.

<div class="verdict-box">
  <div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div>
  <p class="verdict-text">
    <strong>Winner: DALL-E 3 (9.2 vs 8.0), though Flux comes closer than any other open tool.</strong> ChatGPT's prompt interpretation and iterative editing give DALL-E a precision advantage. Flux is already capable enough for most commercial projects.
  </p>
</div>

### Scenario 3: Artistic Style & Creativity

**Test method:** "Art Nouveau travel poster for a space station" and "watercolor illustration of a robot gardening, children's book style."

Both DALL-E 3 images were attractive, clean, professional, and usable. Still, the style felt like an AI imitation of Art Nouveau rather than a convincing poster from the movement.

Flux's base model was in the same range, but LoRA support is what changes the equation. Applying a strong Art Nouveau or watercolor LoRA raised the output from good to excellent, approaching or exceeding Midjourney-level style authenticity. Community LoRAs, fine-tuning, and custom models give Flux a creative ceiling that DALL-E's closed system cannot match.

<div class="verdict-box">
  <div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div>
  <p class="verdict-text">
    <strong>Winner: Flux (8.5 vs 7.5).</strong> Base models are close. Flux's customizability via LoRAs and fine-tuning gives it a higher creative ceiling for users willing to invest the time.
  </p>
